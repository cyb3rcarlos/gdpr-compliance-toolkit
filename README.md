# gdpr-compliance-toolkit
Project Overview

The GDPR Compliance Toolkit is a web-based cybersecurity and data protection solution designed to help Small and Medium-sized Enterprises (SMEs) understand, assess and manage their obligations under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Developed as part of my BSc (Hons) Cyber Security Management degree at Bournemouth University, this project addresses the challenges faced by smaller organisations that lack dedicated cybersecurity teams, compliance specialists and financial resources.

The toolkit aims to bridge the gap between complex regulatory requirements and practical business implementation through accessible guidance, compliance resources and AI-assisted support.

Problem Statement

Many SMEs struggle with GDPR compliance due to:

Limited cybersecurity expertise and resources.

Complex regulatory requirements.

High costs associated with professional compliance consultancy.

Insufficient understanding of data protection responsibilities.

Difficulties implementing appropriate organisational and technical controls.

This project explores how technology can make data protection and regulatory compliance more accessible, manageable and cost-effective.

Project Objectives

Simplify GDPR requirements for SMEs.

Provide accessible data protection guidance.

Support organisations in developing compliance documentation.

Promote cybersecurity awareness and accountability.

Reduce barriers to implementing effective data protection practices.

Demonstrate the practical application of cybersecurity governance, risk and compliance principles.

Key Features

GDPR Compliance Resources: Guidance designed to help organisations understand their regulatory obligations.

Compliance Templates: Documentation resources supporting organisational data protection practices.

AI-Assisted Compliance Support: An interactive assistant designed to help users navigate GDPR-related questions.

Web-Based Interface: A centralised platform for accessing compliance tools and information.

SME-Focused Design: A solution developed with the requirements and resource limitations of smaller organisations in mind.
